What makes a good wooden pallet manufacturer in Mexico?

A wooden pallet manufacturer does more than cut and nail together stringers and deck boards: it designs the right platform for each load, controls lumber quality, guarantees supply and backs every batch with certifications. In a manufacturing country like Mexico, where the pallet is the foundation of nearly the entire supply chain, choosing the right manufacturer directly impacts costs, lead times and customs compliance.

BestPack Solutions is a Mexican wooden pallet manufacturer with a production plant in San Luis Potosí and a distribution warehouse in Saltillo, two key points along the country's industrial corridor. That location puts us close to the main automotive, manufacturing and consumer goods clusters, with optimized freight and short lead times to the Bajío region, the industrial north and the entire country.

Production capacity: the factor that sets pallet manufacturers apart

The most visible difference between pallet manufacturers is capacity. We produce more than 150,000 pallets per month, with installed capacity of up to 350,000, which lets us sustain weekly supply programs for large plants with no risk of shortages while also handling one-time orders. This industrial scale is what backs our relationships with clients such as Daikin, Magna, Bimbo, L'Oréal, Daltile and Contitech.

New, recycled and export pallets

As manufacturers we offer the full line so every operation uses the right pallet. New wooden pallets deliver maximum strength and service life; recycled pallets cut cost and environmental footprint for internal flows; and export pallets with heat treatment (HT) cross any border in compliance with phytosanitary requirements. We also build wooden crates for large, heavy or export parts.

BestPack Total Warranty: we repair pallets at no cost for our clients, extending their service life and protecting your long-term investment. That is what separates a manufacturer from a mere supplier.

Certifications: the guarantee of a serious pallet manufacturer

Certifications are objective proof that a manufacturer operates to standards. We hold ISO 9001 (quality), ISO 14001 (environment) and ISO 45001 (safety), plus NOM-144-SEMARNAT and ISPM 15 for export pallets. Heat treatment (HT) with IPPC stamp eliminates wood pests and allows international transit in compliance with phytosanitary requirements at customs.

A pallet manufacturer with nationwide coverage

We operate from San Luis Potosí and Saltillo with fast deliveries throughout the country. If you need a nearby supplier, we directly serve pallets in San Luis Potosí, pallets in Querétaro, pallets in Guanajuato and pallets in Saltillo, with the same capacity, quality and backing in every area.
