Packaging engineering

How do we build each case study?

A strong case study doesn't start with a catalog product; it starts with an operational challenge. That's why our engineering team designs the solution around your product, your logistics route and your volume.

Challenge diagnosis

We analyze product, weight, route, frequency and the critical damage or cost points in your supply chain.

Design and prototype

We propose the solution — pallet, box, returnable or dunnage — and validate prototypes before production.

Production and certification

We manufacture backed by ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001, NOM-144 and ISPM 15 (HT) where applicable.

Supply and continuous improvement

We ensure consistent deliveries throughout Mexico and adjust the solution as your operation evolves.
