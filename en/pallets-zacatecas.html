BestPack Solutions, pallet manufacturer for Zacatecas

Zacatecas combines three economic engines that depend on reliable packaging: mining in the historic Fresnillo district—led by Fresnillo plc and Industrias Peñoles, the world's largest silver producer—an agribusiness sector that makes the state Mexico's number one producer of beans, garlic and industrial grapes, and an expanding manufacturing base concentrated in the Calera industrial parks. Each one needs different pallets, and all of them need to arrive on time.

BestPack Solutions supplies Zacatecas from its San Luis Potosí plant, the closest to the state: about 2 hours via Federal Highway 49. That proximity lets us schedule deliveries to Zacatecas City, Guadalupe, Fresnillo and the Calera industrial parks with short lead times and continuous supply, so your operation never depends on improvised seasonal vendors.

Calera industrial parks: aerospace, automotive and ceramics

The Zacatecas–Calera corridor concentrates the state's new manufacturing base. Parque Industrial Aeropuerto, on the Zacatecas–Fresnillo highway, is home to aerospace and automotive companies such as Triumph Group de México, Koide México and Johnson Electric, while Parque Industrial Calera brings together ceramics and beverage firms such as Cesantoni and Grupo Modelo. We manufacture pallets with dimensional precision and high load capacity for these supply chains, including sizes built to each plant's specification.

Pallets and crates for Zacatecas agribusiness

Zacatecas is Mexico's leading producer of beans, garlic and industrial grapes, and ranks among the top states for lettuce, tomatillo, guava, peaches and green chili peppers—products increasingly shipped to markets in Asia and North America. We supply wooden crates and pallets sized for harvest collection, stacking and shipping, built to withstand handling in the field, in cold storage and at the export dock.

Heat-treated (HT) export pallets

The mining concentrates, auto parts and agricultural products Zacatecas ships abroad require pallets that comply with NOM-144-SEMARNAT and the international ISPM 15 standard. Our export pallets are heat-treated (HT) and marked in accordance with these standards, preventing customs holds on shipments to the United States, Asia and Europe.

BestPack Total Warranty: we repair pallets at no cost for our customers, extending their service life and protecting your investment throughout the entire operation.
