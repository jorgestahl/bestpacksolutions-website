BestPack Solutions, pallet manufacturer for Querétaro and the Bajío

Querétaro has become one of Mexico's fastest-growing manufacturing hubs, driven by its automotive and aerospace clusters and a network of first-class industrial parks: El Marqués, the Bernardo Quintana area, the Intercontinental Airport corridor and the industrial areas of San Juan del Río. Operating in this environment calls for a pallet supplier that understands the region's just-in-time pace.

BestPack Solutions supplies Querétaro plants with current production of more than 150,000 pallets per month and installed capacity of up to 350,000, shipping from our production plant in San Luis Potosí and our distribution warehouse in Saltillo. That geographic position lets us cover the Bajío corridor with short lead times and continuous supply programs.

Automotive and aerospace pallets built to spec

Querétaro's automotive and aerospace supply chains work to tight tolerances. We manufacture pallets with dimensional precision and high load capacity, validated against the requirements of OEMs and Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers, including special sizes and designs for high-value components.

Heat-treated (HT) export pallets

Bajío exporters need pallets that comply with NOM-144-SEMARNAT and the international ISPM 15 standard. Our export pallets are heat-treated (HT) and marked in accordance with these standards, avoiding customs holds on shipments to North America, Europe and Asia.

BestPack Total Warranty: we repair pallets at no cost to our customers, extending their service life and protecting your investment throughout the entire operation.

Coverage across the state of Querétaro

We serve companies in Querétaro City, El Marqués, San Juan del Río, Pedro Escobedo and the rest of the state, as well as the Bajío industrial corridor. Explore our line of wooden pallets or request a tailored proposal through the contact section.
