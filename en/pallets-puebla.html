BestPack Solutions: pallets for Puebla's industrial cluster

Puebla is one of Mexico's three largest automotive hubs. Between January and November 2025 alone it produced more than 455,000 vehicles, with close to 80% of its industry geared toward export. Driving that activity are the Volkswagen plant in Cuautlancingo—the brand's largest outside Germany—and Audi's plant in San José Chiapa, surrounded by more than 70 Tier 1 suppliers and some 220 Tier 2 and specialized logistics companies.

That ecosystem runs on just-in-time and just-in-sequence flows. Operating here demands reliable, dimensionally precise pallets available at exactly the right moment. BestPack Solutions supplies Puebla with current production of more than 150,000 pallets per month and installed capacity of up to 350,000, from our production plant in San Luis Potosí, about 5-6 hours by road.

Industrial parks in Puebla we supply

We serve the Puebla Valley industrial corridor and its flagship parks: FINSA Puebla—the first automotive supplier park in Latin America, with direct access to the Volkswagen plant—Parque Industrial Bralemex in Cuautlancingo, Parque Industrial Chachapa (auto parts, metalworking and pharma), Ciudad Textil in Huejotzingo, and the industrial zones toward San José Chiapa and Tehuacán.

Supply programs and volume projects

Because Puebla is far from our plant, our offer is built on recurring supply programs, consolidated shipments and agreed safety stock. Freight cost is spread across the volume, and your OEM or Tier 1 operation receives pallets on time, week after week, without relying on rush orders that raise costs and delay operations.

Heat-treated (HT) export pallets

With 80% of Puebla's output bound for foreign markets, export pallets are essential. Ours are heat-treated (HT) and marked in compliance with NOM-144-SEMARNAT and the international ISPM 15 standard, preventing customs holds on shipments leaving through Veracruz for North America, Europe and Asia.

BestPack Total Warranty: we repair pallets at no cost for our customers, extending their service life and protecting your investment throughout the entire operation.

We serve companies in Puebla City, Cuautlancingo, San José Chiapa, Huejotzingo, Tehuacán, Atlixco and the rest of the state. Learn more about our pallet manufacturing or request a custom proposal from the contact section.
