Reinforced pallets for the steel and auto parts industries

Nuevo León is home to some of the country's most important steel complexes and makes close to 40% of Mexico's auto parts. For these sectors we produce reinforced, high-load-capacity pallets, as well as dimensionally precise pallets for the automotive and white-goods supply chains that serve OEMs and Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers.

Heat-treated (HT) export pallets for shipments to the United States

A large share of Monterrey's output crosses the border through Nuevo Laredo and Colombia, one of the country's main export corridors. Our export pallets are heat-treated (HT) and marked in compliance with NOM-144-SEMARNAT and the international ISPM 15 standard, which prevents customs holds on shipments of steel, auto parts and appliances into the United States and Canada.
