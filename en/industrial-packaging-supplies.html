How do industrial packaging supplies help protect your operation?

Good packaging doesn't end with the box or the pallet: industrial packaging supplies are what actually secure, stabilize and protect your product during transport and storage. Loose stretch wrap, strapping without tension or a missing desiccant can ruin a shipment that was otherwise well assembled. That's why choosing the right supply is as important as choosing the right box.

At BestPack Solutions, supplying packaging consumables is part of our integrated industrial packaging solutions. We combine stretch film, plastic and steel strapping, packaging tapes, edge protectors and cushioning materials so every pallet leaves stable, protected and ready to travel, regardless of the distance or the mode of transport.

Protection against moisture, corrosion and static

When the product is sensitive, supplies make the difference. Silica gel desiccants control moisture inside the package to prevent mold and condensation; VCI bags release corrosion inhibitors that protect metal parts; and ESD bags dissipate the electrostatic discharge that damages circuit boards and electronic components. Small details that prevent big losses.
