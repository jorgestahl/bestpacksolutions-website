Pallets for Eje 140, the backbone of the San Luis Potosí Industrial Zone

Eje 140 is one of the main structural roads of the San Luis Potosí Industrial Zone. It connects to Federal Highway 57 and provides access to several of the city's industrial parks —including Tres Naciones, WTC and Parque Logístico— making it a key artery for manufacturing and logistics along the corridor. Operating on this axis calls for a pallet supplier that understands the area's just-in-time flow.

Along Eje 140 and the parks it links, companies from a range of industries operate: auto parts, such as PEASA Autopartes; steel products for the automotive sector, such as Productos Especializados de Acero; containers, such as Envases del Plata in Tres Naciones; plus plastic injection plants, wire-harness assembly and logistics operators. That mix of weights, formats and shipping standards demands a broad, made-to-spec pallet catalog.

A corridor in constant modernization

Eje 140 itself has undergone road widening and modernization projects to improve access to the industrial parks and relieve congestion on Highway 57. For the plants located there, having a pallet supplier that manufactures in the same city reduces dependence on long-haul freight and ensures fast replenishment during any production peak.

Heat-treated (HT) export pallets

Many of the plants along Eje 140 export auto parts and manufactured goods to North America. Those shipments need pallets that comply with NOM-144-SEMARNAT and the international ISPM 15 standard. Our export pallets are heat-treated (HT) and marked in accordance with these standards, avoiding holds at customs.
