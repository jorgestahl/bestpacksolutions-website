Where We Serve You From

BestPack operates a production plant in San Luis Potosí and a distribution warehouse in Saltillo. Pallets, corrugated boxes and crating are manufactured in San Luis Potosí; our warehousing and 3PL services are also operated in San Luis Potosí, with the Saltillo warehouse serving as an additional distribution point for the north.

From our production plant we serve industrial projects across the Bajío region and Central-Western Mexico, including San Luis Potosí, Querétaro, Guanajuato, Aguascalientes, Jalisco and Zacatecas, as well as projects elsewhere in Mexico depending on volume, specification and logistics feasibility.

What We Can Supply Regionally

New, recycled and hybrid wooden pallets; corrugated boxes and corrugated packaging solutions with up to 4-color printing; returnable packaging designed and integrated per project; and industrial packaging supplies under managed supply programs. Companies in any state can also contract our warehousing and 3PL operation in San Luis Potosí to hold inventory, prepare orders or serve customers and plants along the San Luis Potosí industrial corridor.
