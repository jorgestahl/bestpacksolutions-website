Primary, secondary and tertiary packaging: where we fit in

Primary packaging is in direct contact with the medicine (blister, bottle, ampoule) and is governed by specific regulatory requirements. Secondary packaging groups and protects the already-packaged product: corrugated boxes, inserts, dividers. Tertiary packaging prepares it for warehousing and transport: pallets, crating, stretch film, corner boards and strapping. BestPack specializes primarily in the secondary and tertiary levels.

Common challenges we solve

  • Protecting finished product and preventing damage during handling and transport.
  • Dimensional consistency of packaging for automated lines and warehouses.
  • Load stability when stacked, racked and in transit.
  • Identification and printing on secondary packaging (up to 4-color printing).
  • Supply continuity and inventory control of packaging materials (VMI).
  • Supplier consolidation: one partner for wood, corrugated, returnable packaging, supplies and warehousing.
