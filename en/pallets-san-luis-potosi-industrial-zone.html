Pallets for the historic Industrial Zone of San Luis Potosí

The Industrial Zone is the original manufacturing district of the San Luis Potosí capital: an area of roughly 3,800 hectares on the east side of the city, connected to Federal Highway 57 and the Anillo Periférico Oriente, and organized internally by a grid of numbered avenues (Eje 100, 110, 114, 122, 124 and up to Eje 140) around Av. CFE. Much of the zone falls under postal code 78395.

Together with the newer industrial parks, it is the municipality's economic engine: it concentrates hundreds of companies, generates around 180,000 jobs and accounts for close to three quarters of the capital's economic output. Micro and small workshops share the zone with mid-size, large and export plants in the metalworking, steel, food and automotive sectors.

A pallet supplier that understands how the zone operates

Each of those profiles needs a different pallet. BestPack Solutions, as a pallet manufacturer with a production plant in San Luis Potosí, supplies companies along the Ejes and Av. CFE with new wooden pallets, recycled pallets, wooden crates and custom units, adjusting volumes and specifications to each production line.

Heat-treated (HT) export pallets

Many plants in the Industrial Zone are integrated into global supply chains and export on a regular basis. For them we manufacture export pallets with heat treatment (HT) and marking compliant with NOM-144-SEMARNAT and the international ISPM 15 standard, avoiding customs holds on shipments to North America, Europe and Asia.

What types of pallets do you supply to the Industrial Zone?

We manufacture new wooden pallets, recycled pallets, heat-treated (HT) export pallets, wooden crates and custom pallets. We serve the zone's leading sectors: metalworking, steel, food and automotive.

Can these pallets be used for export from the SLP Industrial Zone?
