Why is packaging strategic in the automotive industry?

The automotive industry runs on tight tolerances and synchronized flows, where the wrong packaging can cause damage, rework or, in the worst case, a line stoppage. That is why automotive packaging is designed as part of the production process: it must protect the part, allow ergonomic handling, optimize space in transit and enable deliveries at the exact moment they are needed. BestPack Solutions supports OEMs and suppliers with a portfolio that ranges from the pallet to the returnable container.

Returnable packaging and lower cost per cycle

Returnable packaging (totes, bins, racks and ESD solutions) turns recurring spend on consumables into an investment that pays for itself cycle after cycle. Beyond cutting costs, it reduces waste and improves traceability of the flow between supplier and plant. Our team sizes the container fleet and designs custom inserts for each component to maximize density and protection.

JIT / JIS deliveries and line continuity

We run Just in Time (JIT) and Just in Sequence (JIS) programs from our production plant in San Luis Potosí and our distribution warehouse in Saltillo, which lets us feed production lines with the right packaging, in the right sequence, at the right time. Proximity to the main automotive corridors of the Bajío and northern Mexico is key to sustaining that synchronization.

Export without setbacks: our wood packaging is heat-treated (HT) in compliance with NOM-144 and ISPM 15, so your auto parts cross the border meeting every phytosanitary requirement.

Experience with Mexico's automotive cluster

Mexico is one of the world's leading automotive manufacturing platforms, with established clusters in the Bajío (Guanajuato, Querétaro, Aguascalientes, San Luis Potosí) and in the north (Coahuila, Nuevo León). BestPack Solutions has worked with customers in this sector, delivering packaging solutions that combine current production of more than 150,000 pallets per month with installed capacity of up to 350,000, international certifications and engineering applied to every operation.

Packaging for the automotive industry

What type of packaging do you offer for the automotive industry?

We offer automotive pallets built to spec, returnable packaging (totes, bins, racks and ESD solutions), boxes, protective dunnage and heat-treated (HT) export crating for auto parts in compliance with NOM-144 and ISPM 15.

Do you make JIT or JIS deliveries to production lines?

Yes. We coordinate Just in Time (JIT) and Just in Sequence (JIS) deliveries from our production plant in San Luis Potosí and our distribution warehouse in Saltillo to keep automotive production lines running without interruption.

Can the packaging be used to export auto parts?

Yes. We manufacture heat-treated (HT) wood packaging in compliance with NOM-144 and ISPM 15, valid for exporting auto parts and components while meeting phytosanitary requirements at customs.

Do you offer packaging engineering to reduce costs?

Yes. Our packaging engineering team designs solutions that optimize product protection, improve ergonomics and reduce cost per cycle through returnable packaging and better use of space.

Which regions of the automotive cluster do you serve?

We serve Mexico's automotive cluster in the Bajío and the north from our production plant in San Luis Potosí and our distribution warehouse in Saltillo, with nationwide coverage for OEMs and Tier 1 suppliers.
