BestPack Solutions, your pallet manufacturer in Aguascalientes

Aguascalientes is one of Mexico's most industrialized states relative to its size, with more than 20 industrial parks spread across the capital, Jesús María, Calvillo and San Francisco de los Romo. The engine is the automotive cluster led by Nissan, surrounded by an ecosystem of Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers running at the demanding just-in-time pace of the Douki Seisan system. Supplying pallets in this environment calls for a pallet supplier in Mexico that never misses a single shipment.

BestPack Solutions supplies Aguascalientes plants with current production of more than 150,000 pallets per month and installed capacity of up to 350,000, shipped from our production plant in San Luis Potosí. That proximity — roughly 2 hours via Highways 70 and 45 — lets us offer scheduled deliveries and continuous supply without relying on long-haul freight or idle inventory tying up space in your facility.

Industrial parks in Aguascalientes we supply

We deliver to the state's main developments: Parque Industrial San Francisco I, II and III in San Francisco de los Romo — the automotive and metalworking epicenter — the Douki Seisan Park (DSP) for Nissan suppliers developed by Vesta, the new Vesta parks, as well as PILA (Logística Automotriz), PIVA (Valle de Aguascalientes), FINSA and El Vergel. We coordinate routes and dock schedules tailored to each plant.

Automotive pallets built to the specifications of Nissan and its supply base

The Aguascalientes automotive chain works to tight tolerances. We manufacture pallets with dimensional precision and high load capacity, validated for the requirements of OEMs and Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers — from seating and wire-harness manufacturers to steel processors located in San Francisco de los Romo — including special sizes and designs for high-value components.

Heat-treated (HT) export pallets and crates

Aguascalientes exports billions of dollars a year, led by the transportation sector, to destinations in the United States, Europe and Asia. Those shipments need packaging that complies with NOM-144-SEMARNAT and the international ISPM 15 standard. Our export pallets and wooden crates are heat-treated (HT) and marked in accordance with these standards, which prevents holds at customs.
