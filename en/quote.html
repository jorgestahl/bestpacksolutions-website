Before you request a quote

Frequently asked questions

Do you sell and manufacture wooden pallets?

Yes. We manufacture new, recycled and hybrid wooden pallets at our plant in San Luis Potosí and sell them directly to companies, in the standard 48x40 and 48x45 sizes or custom-sized for your load. For volume purchases, indicate quantity and frequency in the form.

Do you accept one-time pallet orders or only continuous supply?

Both. We handle everything from a single order to continuous supply programs with scheduled deliveries. Indicate the approximate volume and frequency in the form so we can quote according to your operation.

Do I need HT treatment to export?

Yes. Wood packaging for export must have HT heat treatment in compliance with NOM-144 and ISPM-15, with the IPPC stamp. If your shipment is for export, select Wooden pallets and mention it in the description.

What information do you need to quote?

Product or size (for example 48x40), the load it must support, whether you need it new, recycled or hybrid, approximate volume and delivery city or plant. If you have a drawing or specification, you can attach it.
