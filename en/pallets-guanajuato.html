BestPack Solutions, pallet manufacturer for Guanajuato

Guanajuato is the heart of the Bajío automotive cluster and one of Mexico's most industrialized states. Silao and Celaya concentrate OEMs and auto-parts suppliers; Salamanca and Irapuato add chemical, petrochemical and agribusiness activity; and León is the country's leading leather and footwear hub. Each of these sectors has its own packaging and pallet requirements.

BestPack Solutions supplies the entire state with current production of more than 150,000 pallets per month and installed capacity of up to 350,000, shipping from our production plant in San Luis Potosí and our distribution warehouse in Saltillo. This logistics base lets us cover the Bajío with short lead times and continuous supply programs.

Automotive pallets for the Silao–Celaya corridor

The automotive sector's just-in-time model doesn't tolerate shortages. We manufacture pallets with dimensional precision and high load capacity, validated for the requirements of OEMs and Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers in Silao, Celaya, Irapuato and Salamanca, with special sizes and designs made to order.

Heat-treated (HT) export pallets

Guanajuato exporters need pallets that comply with NOM-144-SEMARNAT and the international ISPM 15 standard. Our export pallets are heat-treated (HT) and marked in accordance with these standards, avoiding customs holds on shipments to North America, Europe and Asia.

BestPack Total Warranty: we repair pallets at no cost for our clients, extending their service life and protecting your investment throughout the entire operation.
