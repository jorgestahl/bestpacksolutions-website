The area

What companies and industries operate in Parque Logistik?

Parque Industrial Logistik sits about 25 km south of the state capital, in the municipality of Villa de Reyes, with direct access to Federal Highway 57 and Federal Highway 80, which links the Gulf of Mexico with the Pacific. It is one of the most important industrial hubs in the country and operates its own inland customs facility and freight terminal.

Automotive OEMs

The park is home to world-class assembly plants such as BMW Group and General Motors, the anchor of the region's entire supply chain.

Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers

Companies such as Continental, Faurecia, POSCO and Minghua make up the supplier base that serves the OEMs within the same park.

Logistics and distribution

About 20% of the companies in the park run logistics and distribution operations, taking advantage of the inland customs facility and freight terminal.

Parque Logistik brings together around 70 companies and more than 27,000 employees, with close to 80% in the automotive and manufacturing sectors. This ecosystem on Highway 57 demands a constant flow of pallets and wooden packaging for production, crating and export.

BestPack Solutions: Pallets for Parque Industrial Logistik

Parque Industrial Logistik has become one of the largest industrial complexes in Mexico. Located in the municipality of Villa de Reyes, about 25 km south of the city of San Luis Potosí and directly connected to Federal Highway 57 (the trade backbone toward North America) and Federal Highway 80, it is home to around 70 companies and more than 27,000 employees. Operating as a supplier in this environment demands proximity, capacity and certifications.

As a manufacturer with a local plant in San Luis Potosí, BestPack Solutions supplies pallets and wooden packaging to Logistik with short lead times and continuous supply programs. Our proximity to the park sets us apart from suppliers that depend on freight from other states: we replenish inventory quickly and keep pace with the automotive cluster's production peaks.

Automotive pallets built to each plant's specification

Logistik is anchored by world-class OEMs such as BMW and General Motors and by Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers such as Continental and Faurecia, all working to strict tolerances. We manufacture pallets with dimensional precision and high load capacity, plus wooden crates and custom packaging for auto parts and high-value components.

Export pallets with an inland customs facility in the park

Parque Logistik has an inland customs facility and freight terminal, which makes exporting a daily operation. Our export pallets are heat-treated (HT) and marked in compliance with NOM-144-SEMARNAT and the international ISPM 15 standard, avoiding customs holds on shipments to North America, Europe and Asia.
