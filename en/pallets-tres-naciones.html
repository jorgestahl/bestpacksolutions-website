Which companies and industries operate in Parque Tres Naciones?

Parque Industrial Tres Naciones was the first private industrial park in San Luis Potosí. With more than 20 years in operation, it is now home to around 50 companies from different countries that generate more than 13,000 jobs in the Industrial Zone.

Located on Federal Highway 57 (km 187.3) and Eje 140, in the Industrial Zone of the state capital, Tres Naciones is one of the most recognized industrial parks in the state. Its dominant sector is automotive and auto parts, alongside metalworking, plastics and logistics companies. Plants historically established in the park include firms such as Cummins, Valeo, Eaton, Remy, Edscha, Hitchiner and Polymer Tech, among others.

For all of them, the pallet is not just another consumable: it is part of the production line, the auto-parts packaging and the export shipments that leave via Highway 57 toward northern Mexico and the United States. That is why working with a pallet supplier located inside San Luis Potosí makes the difference between a line that keeps moving and one that stops for lack of pallets.
