What is a logistics & 3PL service?

A 3PL (third-party logistics provider) is an outside company that runs supply chain processes on your behalf: warehousing, cross-docking, picking and packing, repackaging, labeling, subassemblies, shipment preparation and inventory management. Instead of investing in warehouses, staff and systems, you hire a specialist that operates those processes efficiently and at a variable cost.

At BestPack Solutions, logistics and 3PL are not a standalone service: they are integrated with the manufacturing of your packaging. The same supplier that designs your pallets, boxes or returnable packaging also warehouses, repackages and prepares your shipments, all from our production plant in San Luis Potosí and our distribution warehouse in Saltillo, with nationwide coverage across Mexico.

Packaging + 3PL: one operation, lower costs

Integrating packaging with logistics creates a virtuous cycle. On one hand, you eliminate the cost of coordinating multiple suppliers and reduce rework, errors and idle time: the operation becomes more efficient. On the other, the relationship shifts from a one-off packaging purchase to an ongoing service—warehousing, VMI, repackaging—with a single accountable party and clear service levels: your operation gains supply certainty and stable costs over the long term.
