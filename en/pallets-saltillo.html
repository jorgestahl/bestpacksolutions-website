Why choose BestPack for wooden pallets in Saltillo?

Saltillo and its metro area form one of the most important automotive clusters in Mexico. With OEMs and a dense network of suppliers in Ramos Arizpe and Derramadero, the region's supply chain demands a packaging supplier that combines proximity, capacity and compliance. That is why BestPack Solutions operates a distribution warehouse in Saltillo: holding local inventory lets us shorten response times and sustain high-volume programs, with production at our plant in San Luis Potosí.

Pallets, returnable packaging and export crating from a single supplier

Beyond the pallet, we are an integrated packaging partner. We supply new, recycled and automotive wooden pallets built to spec; we design returnable packaging systems that lower cost per cycle; and we produce heat-treated export packaging. Consolidating these services with one supplier that has a warehouse in Coahuila simplifies logistics and improves traceability across your supply chain.

Auto parts export with NOM-144 / ISPM 15 compliance

Exports from Coahuila to the United States require wood packaging treated in accordance with NOM-144-SEMARNAT and the international ISPM 15 standard. All of our export pallets and crates receive heat treatment (HT) and the corresponding marking, so your product meets the phytosanitary requirements to cross the border.
