BestPack Solutions: pallets for Guadalajara and its metropolitan area

Guadalajara is the heart of Mexico's largest electronics cluster and one of the most dynamic logistics hubs in the western part of the country. The El Salto corridor —the most active in the metro area, home to contract-manufacturing giants such as Foxconn and suppliers such as Aptiv— concentrates the highest demand for industrial space. Next door, Tlajomulco de Zúñiga hosts dozens of industrial distribution and last-mile complexes, while the Guadalajara Technology Park in Zapopan brings together electronics, appliance and consumer-goods plants. Operating in this environment demands a pallet supplier that understands the just-in-time pace of electronics and export.

BestPack Solutions supplies Guadalajara-area plants with a current production of more than 150,000 pallets per month and an installed capacity of up to 350,000. We deliver to Guadalajara from our plant in San Luis Potosí, located roughly 5 hours away by highway, with continuous supply programs that keep your line stocked.

Packaging for the electronics cluster: ESD and returnable

High-value electronics in El Salto need more than a pallet: they require a packaging system that protects electrostatic-discharge (ESD) sensitive components during handling and transport. We design custom pallets, crates and packaging that integrate with industrial returnable packaging programs, reducing scrap and cost per cycle in contract-manufacturing operations.

Heat-treated (HT) export pallets

Guadalajara's electronics industry, along with the agribusiness and tequila of Los Altos de Jalisco, exports to the United States, Asia and Europe. Those shipments need pallets that comply with NOM-144-SEMARNAT and the international ISPM 15 standard. Our export pallets and wooden crates are heat-treated (HT) and marked in accordance with these standards, which prevents holds at customs.

BestPack Total Warranty: we repair pallets at no cost for our customers, extending their service life and protecting your investment throughout your entire operation.

Coverage in the Guadalajara metropolitan area

We serve companies in Guadalajara, Zapopan, El Salto, Tlajomulco de Zúñiga, Tonalá and the rest of the metropolitan area, as well as the corridors of Los Altos de Jalisco.
